How to use this together
  1. Choose a module and station. Candidate reads only the Candidate view; partner opens Patient on a second device or uses a printed script. Use the same station code on both devices.
  2. Partner volunteers only the opening and visible behavior. Reveal answers when relevant questions are asked; natural equivalent wording counts. Give examination findings only after the candidate describes an appropriate technique.
  3. Do not read interpretation, diagnostic names or scoring hints aloud. If a detail is not supplied, say “That information is not provided” and let the candidate explain how they would obtain it. Do not invent reassuring negatives.
  4. Use Examiner afterward to discuss 3–5 differentials, investigations, safety actions and the checklist. There is no official pass mark. Repeat with roles reversed, ideally using a different station.
  5. Use Print to make separate candidate and patient sheets. Full pack includes answers; candidate-only printing keeps the script and diagnoses out. Download this workbook for offline use on your Mac; external references and sound clips still need internet.

These are authored study adaptations, not official final stations or a prediction of the exam. Added findings are labelled per station. The supplied 2011 OSCE handbook informed examination structure; current linked sources inform selected clinical updates. Your instructor’s rubric and current local protocols take precedence for actual assessment/care. Module 1 here is AHAD foundations.

Practise verbalizing or safely simulating techniques. Intimate, invasive, painful, pressure-on-eye and carotid maneuvers use supplied findings rather than being performed on your partner.

Print, save or share

Print dialogs can save as PDF on Mac or Windows. Marks stay in this browser; exporting lets you move them between devices. The workbook has its own score storage, separate from Study Grove question progress. A shared link always opens in Candidate view.
